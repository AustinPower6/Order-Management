Order Management System – User Guide

Welcome! This guide describes how to operate the Order Management System – a multi-tenant order and invoice manager for small businesses. It covers the full document chain from quote to reminder, the master data, PDF printing, e-invoicing and email sending.

You can open this guide from anywhere in the application with the F1 key. The jump takes you straight to the chapter for the section you currently have open.

Unsaved changes are shown in dialogs by a red dot in front of the Save button. "Cancel" discards the changes and reloads the original state; closing again (Esc/X) closes the dialog.

Navigation & sidebar

The application is organised into tabs. The left sidebar and the top menu give access to every area: master data (company, customers, articles), the documents (quotes, orders, delivery notes, invoices, reminders) as well as reports, the email outbox and settings.

The active company (tenant) is shown in the sidebar and can be switched there – see Company management. Window size, column widths and tab order are saved automatically and restored on the next start.

Master data — overview

Before creating documents, maintain the master data. A sensible order:

  1. Company master – your own company data, tax, bank, fiscal years, number ranges.
  2. VAT classes – tax rates (e.g. standard 19 %, reduced 7 %).
  3. Customers – your clients.
  4. Articles – your products/services.

All master data and documents always belong to the active company. With several companies (tenants) the data is strictly separated.

Company master

Master data → Company master

The company master bundles all details of your own company across several tabs:

  • Address – name, address, font of the company name on documents.
  • Parameters – tax/VAT ID, bank details, email client, e-invoice.
  • Fiscal years – per year with chart of accounts (SKR 03/04).
  • Number ranges – document numbers, customer/general/creditor accounts per fiscal year.
  • VAT, payment and reminder terms, base interest rate.
  • Print texts, standard texts, email texts, signatures, copies, layout, paths.

Required before the first document: company name, address, tax data, bank details and at least one fiscal year with a document number range.

Customers

Master data → Customers

Record your clients here with address, contact data, VAT ID and optionally a fixed payment and reminder term. The customer number is assigned automatically from the number range (debtors) defined in the company master.

  • New / Edit / Delete via the button bar.
  • In documents you pick the customer through a selection dialog (search, Enter/double-click).

Articles

Master data → Articles

Articles are your products and services with article number, description, price, unit and VAT class. You can also assign:

  • Brand (with logo), product group, article/sub group.
  • An article image and a description.

When inserted into a document, the current article price and the VAT rate valid at the document date are taken over and frozen into the line item.

VAT classes

Master data → VAT

A VAT class (e.g. "standard") holds several time-dated rates. When a line item is created, the rate valid at the document date is frozen into the item – so historical documents stay correct even after a tax change.

Terms

In the company master you define reusable terms that are assigned to documents:

Payment terms

Payment deadlines and discount (e.g. "14 days net", "30 days / 2 % discount within 8 days"). They determine the due date on the invoice.

Reminder terms

Define the reminder levels (1–4) with due days, reminder fee and interest per level.

Base interest rate

Time-dated base interest rates as the basis for the default-interest calculation in reminders.

Workflow (document chain)

Documents build on each other. From one document you create the next via the buttons in the respective list:

FromActionResult
Quote"→ Order"Order (quote set to "accepted")
Order"→ Delivery note"Delivery note
Order"→ Invoice"Invoice (skips delivery note)
Delivery note"→ Invoice"Invoice
Invoice"→ Reminder"Reminder (in levels up to 4)

Line items and data are carried over and can be edited further in the new document. The PDF and the document-chain dialog show all linked predecessor and successor documents.

Document numbers

Document numbers are assigned automatically and sequentially per document type and fiscal year (format e.g. RE2026-0001). You configure the counters in the company master under Number ranges.

Deleting & restoring

Documents are soft-deleted (marked as deleted, not removed) and can be restored via "Show deleted". Finalised invoices are not deleted but cancelled.

Editing documents

All document types share the same operation: a list with a month/year filter and an edit form. In the form you enter the head (customer, date, subject, texts) and the line items.

Line items

In the line-item editor you enter per row: description, long text, quantity, unit, unit price, VAT class and discount. Articles can be taken from the master.

  • Change the order with Up/Down.
  • Net, VAT (grouped by rate) and gross are calculated automatically.
  • The VAT rate is frozen to the document date.

Quotes

Documents → Quotes

The quote is the first document of the chain. After the customer accepts it, create the follow-up with "→ Order"; the quote status changes to "accepted".

Orders

Documents → Orders

The order documents the binding purchase. From here you create either a delivery note or an invoice directly.

Delivery notes

Documents → Delivery notes

The delivery note accompanies the goods (in the standard layout possible without prices). From it you create the invoice.

Invoices

Documents → Invoices

The invoice closes the process. On the first real print it is finalised (creation date set) and can no longer be edited.

Cancellation: a finalised invoice is not deleted but cancelled. This automatically creates a cancellation invoice with negative amounts. A corrected version can then be edited further as a copy.

Reminders

Documents → Reminders

For an overdue invoice, create reminders step by step via "→ Reminder" (up to level 4). Reminder fees and default interest result from the reminder terms and the base interest rate.

Standard texts & markers

Company master → Standard texts

Per document type you store an upper and lower standard text, which is pre-filled into "Text above" / "Text below" for new documents. The texts remain freely editable in the document.

Markers

Standard texts may contain markers of the form {Prefix+Suffix} that are replaced by real values on printing.

PrefixMeaning
AN / AU / LS / RE / MAQuote / Order / Delivery note / Invoice / Reminder
SuffixMeaning
NRDocument number
DATUMDocument date
GESAMTGross amount
FÄLLIGDue date
FTAGEDays until due

Examples: {RENR} = invoice number, {REFÄLLIG} = invoice due date, {LSDATUM} = delivery-note date. Values are derived through the document chain from predecessor documents; markers that cannot be resolved become "(—)".

Printing

Documents are generated as PDF, saved in the folder Ausdrucke/{Year}/{Month}/{Day}/ (or the configured export path) and opened automatically.

Original print of a document

The original print produces the final PDF. For invoices the document is finalised in the process. At the same time – if an email client is configured – an entry is created in the email outbox. If an already printed document is changed afterwards, the list marks the outdated original (red marking) until it is printed again.

Test print

The test print produces a preview PDF (with a notice/watermark) without finalising the document or creating an email entry. Ideal for checking the layout.

Journals

Reports

Via the "Reports" menu you print journals (quote book, order book, delivery-note book, invoice book, reminder book) by month and year.

Multi-user & locking

The application is designed for several simultaneous users. A record someone is editing is locked optimistically; others see the editor and can only change the record after it is released. Locks are released automatically on saving/closing. Active locks can be viewed in the company master under "Locks" (admin).

E-invoice

The application produces electronic invoices per EN 16931 in the formats UBL 2.1, UN/CEFACT CII D16B, XRechnung 3.0 and ZUGFeRD (PDF/A-3 hybrid).

Configuration

Company master → Parameters → E-invoice

Here you choose the desired format and enter the required details (e.g. routing ID for XRechnung). The generated XML/PDF files are stored following the path scheme {Type}/{CompanyNumber}/{Year}/{Month}.

E-invoice spool

Reports → E-invoice spool

The spool lists all generated e-invoice files with status and validation result, so you keep an overview of sent and pending e-invoices.

Email sending

Email outbox

On the original print an email entry with the document as an attachment is created automatically. In the outbox you send these emails via the configured client.

Configuration

Company master → Parameters → Email client

Choose one of the four clients and enter the respective credentials. The email texts (subject/body) are maintained under Company master → Email texts.

Client overview

ClientDelivery method

Brevo

Create an account at brevo.com, generate an API key (Settings → SMTP & API) and enter it in Company master → Parameters. Recommended for reliable cloud sending.

Gmail

Enable two-factor authentication, create an app password (myaccount.google.com/apppasswords) and store it with the Gmail address.

Outlook 365 classic

Requires Outlook 365 classic installed as the default mail client and the pywin32 package. The email is created directly through Outlook.

New Outlook

New Outlook offers no COM interface. The application opens a mailto: link; the attachments are placed in a collection folder and opened in Explorer so you can drag them into the compose window.

Company management (tenants)

The application is multi-tenant: a single database can hold several companies. All documents and master data are strictly separated by a company number – each company sees only its own data. The active company is shown in the sidebar and switched there.

Copy a company

Creates a full copy of an existing company (master data and documents as a template) under a new company number. Useful for quickly setting up another company. An admin function enabled in the settings.

Delete a company

Removes a company and its data. The currently active company cannot be deleted – switch to another company first. Also an admin function enabled in the settings.

Chart of accounts

Company master → Chart of accounts

The chart of accounts (SKR 03 / SKR 04) provides the general-ledger accounts for assigning revenue, purchase and tax accounts per VAT class and fiscal year. The assignment is done in the company master under Number ranges; account names are shown from the chart of accounts and can be selected via a search.

Import / Export

Menu (admin section)

"Export data" saves all tables as a JSON file; "Import data" restores them. The import is cross-version: only columns present both in the JSON file and the current schema are taken over.

Spell checking

Spelling mistakes are underlined in red in text fields. The check language switches automatically with the app language (German/English). Install the dictionaries once with Install_Woerterbuecher.cmd or python Install_Woerterbuecher.py. Without dictionaries the application keeps working without underlining.

Settings

In the menu you set the language (German/English) and design (light/dark). Window position, size and column widths are saved automatically. Certain admin functions (copy/ delete company, show IDs/locks, test mode) are enabled here.

Test mode

In test mode you can set a different document date to try out workflows safely. The mode is clearly marked and should be disabled in live operation.

Database & backup

All data is held in a single SQLite file (app/daten/auftragsabwicklung.db). On start the schema is migrated to the current level automatically; before each migration a backup is created automatically. For your own backup it is enough to copy this file.

Notes & tips

  • In selection lists Enter or a double-click is enough to accept.
  • The red dot indicates unsaved changes.
  • "Cancel" discards changes; the dialog stays open. Esc/X closes it.
  • A red marking in the document list means: the printed original is outdated – please print again.
  • Finalised invoices are not deleted but cancelled.
  • For problems, the error log app/daten/auftragsabwicklung.log helps – hand it to the developer if needed.
